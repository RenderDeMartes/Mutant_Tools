Hi and welcome to Mutant Tools!

Installing tools can be annoying sometimes, but here are a couple of videos to make your life easy:
https://mutanttools.com/installation-guide/

Basically, just copy and paste the Mutant_Tools folder into your Maya scripts folder:
/home/user_name/maya/202X/scripts/Mutant_Tools

You can also drag and drop the easy_install.py file into the viewport to install automatically.
Sometimes it fails, so we usually recommend doing it manually.

We also include a useful shelf file: shelf_Bluetape_Rigging.mel
Copy and paste it into your shelf prefs folder (make sure Maya is closed):
/home/user_name/maya/202X/prefs/shelves

Cheers
